    Roadmap to the Platinum

    About 200 hours in total: 60–80 for the story, around 100 for the 100% checklist, and roughly 10 for cleanup. Difficulty 4/10. One playthrough is enough — there are no difficulty settings and no mission replay, so keep a separate save at the start and end of every chapter.

    1. Step 1 — Story and side missions 60–80 hours

      Play the story and pick up optional missions as they appear (purple icons, plus black icons with pink letters). Along the way, scan every new vehicle with WAiNK and study animals through binoculars. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude — it is the one possibly missable trophy. Start every Stranger chain as soon as it unlocks.

    2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist and Challenges ~100 hours

      After the ending, Progress > Stats gains the 100% Completion entry. Only 70 of the 80 Challenges are needed, most side quests are not required, and collectibles count only partly. You do need every robbery, underwater discovery, shooting range, drone race and Bret quest. Thresholds are lenient — stunts 300 (trophy 250), races won 30 (trophy 20), Challenges 70 (trophy 50), card sets 15 (trophy 3), bank vaults 8 (trophy 5).

    3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup ~10 hours

      The checklist does not ask for these quests, so they are usually what is left, plus any stray trophies that did not pop on their own. Finish the stranger chains, then mop up the last collectibles.

    Before you start

    • Saves: 15 manual slots plus 1 autosave. With no mission replay, keep a separate save at the start and end of every chapter (10 slots) and rotate the other 5 in free roam after the ending.
    • Cheats disable trophies and saving. Cheats only unlock after the story — keep them off the platinum save.
    • Avoid the “Irredeemable” criminal profile: it is permanent and makes police more aggressive.
    • Every character tied to a trophy is still around in free roam after the ending. Jason and Lucia's relationship does not matter for any trophy.

    Missables

    Only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable. Everything else can be earned in free roam after the ending.

    Start stranger chains early

    Stranger mission chains behind five hidden trophies have windows linked to story progress. Begin each chain as soon as it unlocks — Andy (Chapter 3), Kenton (Chapter 1), Billy (Chapter 2), Randy (Chapter 3) and Tyrone (Chapter 3) — rather than leaving them for after the ending.
